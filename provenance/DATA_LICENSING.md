# AQ26 public data licensing and attribution

## UK-AIR / Defra monitoring data

The England AURN evidence redistributed through the governed AQ26 public export is derived from official UK-AIR monitoring information. AQ26 retains source identity, units, time basis, data-status flags and the scientific limitations carried by the governed private release.

Attribution used by AQ26 for UK-AIR-derived material:

> © Crown 2026 copyright Defra via uk-air.defra.gov.uk, licensed under the Open Government Licence.

## Third-party evidence

The AQ26 private engine also tracks regulatory, meteorological, satellite, emissions, traffic, commercial and research sources. Presence in the private source catalogue does not automatically grant public redistribution rights. Raw or transformed third-party records are added to the public allowlist only when their reuse basis is explicit.

## AQ26-authored material

This file records data-source attribution and redistribution boundaries. It does not assign a software or documentation licence to AQ26-authored code or prose. Any repository-wide licence for AQ26-authored material must be selected explicitly by the project owner rather than inferred from upstream data licences.

## Scientific limitation

Permission to redistribute data does not establish source attribution, causation, personal exposure, health impact, permit breach, operator fault or legal non-compliance.
