How to read this page

Scientific evidence and software assurance are different

Scientific evidence

Measurements, station and regional coverage, descriptive calculations, source records and stated limitations are the evidence available for review.

Engineering assurance

Automated checks confirm that the pipeline followed its declared rules. They do not constitute peer review, accreditation, regulatory approval or institutional endorsement.

Operational PM2.5 screening interpretation

Adjusted PM2.5 candidate count: 0; eligible station assessments: 124; not eligible or not assessed: 3. A count of zero means only that no eligible assessment crossed every current operational criterion. It does not establish absence of pollution, harmless concentrations, assessment of every station or screening of all pollutants.

Formal false-discovery-rate control has not been established. The live screen does not identify a source and is not a health assessment or regulatory determination.

Why the monitoring denominators differ

The observatory publishes separate products with separate eligibility gates. Their counts must not be treated as one denominator.

The build acquired official DfT AADF records for 2025, providing annual traffic and vehicle-class baseline context.

Boundary. Annual AADF evidence is not contemporaneous event traffic. Event-specific traffic still requires time-aligned observations such as WebTRIS or equivalent official data before traffic can support an episode-level competing-source assessment.

Operational PM2.5 screen: threshold and multiplicity boundary

The current screen uses operational screening parameters including a 24-hour PM2.5 mean floor of 15 µg/m³ and a robust station-history score of 3 or greater. These are project screening parameters, not UK legal limits, WHO guideline values, health thresholds, or evidence of a pollution source.

Calibration status. Until an empirical null, sensitivity analysis and dependence-aware calibration are published, the project does not claim that the nominal Benjamini–Hochberg alpha establishes a validated false-discovery rate. The scores are screening statistics rather than calibrated p-values.

WHO context is not UK legal compliance

For NO₂, the WHO 2021 annual air-quality guideline is 10 µg/m³, while the UK statutory annual-mean limit value/objective is 40 µg/m³. WHO-context pollutant-day counts must not be interpreted as UK legal exceedance counts.
